01 · Research & Education

What I study, current research focus, and test scores.

Most AI evaluations test whether an LLM can produce a plausible-sounding answer. My research focuses on a more foundational question: can we mathematically verify that an AI's deduction is sound before it is delivered to a user?

With LogicGuard, my co-author Moiz Ali and I adapted Ibn Sina's 11th-century syllogistic logic (Qiyas) into a deterministic premise-validation engine. Evaluated across 525 TruthfulQA benchmarks on open-weight models (LLaMA and Mistral), it caught logical hallucinations with 100% precision and zero false positives.

I am currently extending this logic-first verification model to autonomous multi-agent pipelines and clinical phone operations at Bytelytic and CALLE-AI.
